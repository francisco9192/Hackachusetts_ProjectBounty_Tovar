# Product

## What Bounty is
Bounty is a community-driven web platform (Hackachusetts, Civic Action track) that connects local small businesses and individuals who need digital help (websites, spreadsheets, graphic design, programming, writing, social media) with local students and other skilled people.

## Who it serves
- Small businesses and individuals who cannot easily afford paid digital services.
- High school students (13+), college students, and aspiring professionals who want real-world experience, portfolio pieces, and community connections.

## What makes it distinct
- **Flexible rewards.** A task can offer money, a nonmonetary reward (product, service, experience, or other), or be a volunteer opportunity. Monetary payment is simulated for the demo; no real money moves and no card or bank data is ever collected.
- **Local by design.** Every task and profile belongs to one US state. Members can only apply to and be accepted for tasks in their own state. Work stays in the local community.
- **Safe for minors.** Ages 13+ can join. Users aged 13-17 see a safety notice and their age is never shown publicly. All contact stays in the in-app message thread so emails and phone numbers are not exposed.

## Core workflow (the demo loop)
Post a task -> browse -> apply -> creator accepts one applicant -> applicant submits work -> creator confirms completion -> reward is settled.

## Product priorities (in order)
1. A reliable, working core loop over extra features.
2. Accessibility and plain language for nontechnical users.
3. Polished, trustworthy, responsive UI.
4. Stretch features (recommendations, portfolio, testimonials) only after the deployed MVP works.

## Hard rules
- No paid services or paid external APIs. Everything must be free for hackathon use.
- Never collect real payment credentials.
- Never expose a member's email or age bracket publicly.
